    How this is measured

    A visit is one browser-tab session that viewed a page. A bounce is a visit that viewed one page and clicked nothing. Time counts only while the page is in the foreground. Countries come from the visitor's network location; no IP addresses, cookies or personal data are stored. Browsers that send Do Not Track are not counted, and bots are filtered out.
