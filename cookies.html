Cookie Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Websites use them to work properly, to remember your settings, and to measure how people use the site. Similar technologies, such as local storage and tracking pixels, do the same kinds of jobs. In this policy, "cookies" includes those technologies.

2. Cookies we set

As of the date above, quantidawn.com does not set any cookies and does not use local storage, tracking pixels, analytics or advertising tools. The table shows each category and whether we use it today.

Our team also uses a private dashboard that is not linked from this site and is not for visitors. When a team member signs in, it stores a sign-in session in that person's own browser. It does not track visitors.

Strictly necessaryKeeping the site workingNone

3. Third-party services on our pages

Our pages load typefaces from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests the font files from Google's servers, so Google receives your IP address and basic browser information. We understand that Google Fonts does not set cookies. Read Google's Privacy Policy to learn how Google handles that data.

4. If this changes

If we add analytics or any other cookies, we will list each one on this page (its name, provider, purpose and how long it lasts) and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent before it is set.

5. Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Because we do not currently set any, blocking cookies will not affect how this site works. We do not track you across other websites, and we do not respond to "Do Not Track" signals because there is nothing to turn off.

6. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email hello@quantidawn.com. For how we handle personal information more generally, read our Privacy Policy.
