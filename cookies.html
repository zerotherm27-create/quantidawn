Cookie Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Websites use them to work properly, to remember your settings, and to measure how people use the site. Similar technologies, such as local storage and tracking pixels, do the same kinds of jobs. In this policy, "cookies" includes those technologies.

2. Cookies we set

As of the date above, quantidawn.com does not set any cookies and does not use tracking pixels or advertising tools. We do run our own simple, privacy-friendly analytics, described in section 4. It keeps a random visit ID in your browser tab's session storage (which is deleted when you close the tab), not in a cookie. The table shows each category and whether we use it today.

Our team also uses a private dashboard that is not linked from this site and is not for visitors. When a team member signs in, it stores a sign-in session in that person's own browser. It does not track visitors.

3. Third-party services on our pages

Our pages load typefaces from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests the font files from Google's servers, so Google receives your IP address and basic browser information. We understand that Google Fonts does not set cookies. Read Google's Privacy Policy to learn how Google handles that data.

4. Our analytics, and if this changes

We measure how the site is used with a small script of our own. It records which pages are viewed, which links and buttons are clicked, how long a page is in the foreground, how far down it is scrolled, whether the visit came from another website, your device type (mobile, tablet or desktop), and your country and region as worked out by our hosting provider. We do not store your IP address, we do not use cookies, we do not build a profile of you, and the data is not shared with advertisers. The random visit ID lives in your browser tab's session storage and is deleted when you close the tab, so we cannot recognise you when you come back. We use the results to understand what visitors need and to improve the site.

Our analytics respects the "Do Not Track" and Global Privacy Control signals: if your browser sends either one, nothing is recorded. If we add any other cookies or tracking tools, we will list each one on this page (its name, provider, purpose and how long it lasts) and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent before it is set.

5. Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Because we do not currently set any, blocking cookies will not affect how this site works. We do not track you across other websites, and our analytics stops recording if your browser sends a "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signal.

Questions about this policy? Email info@quantidawn.com. For how we handle personal information more generally, read our Privacy Policy.
