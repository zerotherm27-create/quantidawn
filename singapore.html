Questions from Singapore contractors

What do you need from me to start?

Drawings, specifications and scope notes, uploaded through our intake form. Add anything else that helps us price the job.

How long does an estimate take?

Estimates usually arrive within 5 business days. When you send a request, we come back with a quote within one business day.

Who prepares and checks the estimate?

Active construction estimators measure the quantities and apply market rates. AI assists, but a person verifies every number, and our engineers review every estimate before it goes out.

What kinds of projects do you estimate?

Residential, commercial, civil and infrastructure work, plus trade-specific packages for subcontractors.

Can I use you for a single project or ongoing work?

Both. You can order one estimate at a time or take a monthly retainer. See pricing for the tiers.
