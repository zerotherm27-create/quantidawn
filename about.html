About

Built by people who've done the work.

QuantiDawn started with a simple frustration: good contractors lose good bids because estimating takes too long. We're hands-on estimators and engineers - not a call center - combining real field estimating experience, engineering rigor, and modern tools to get you accurate numbers fast.

Our Team

  • Estimating

    Every project is estimated by our team of active construction estimators, with hands-on experience across residential, commercial, and civil work.

  • Engineering Review

    Every estimate is reviewed for accuracy by our engineers before it reaches you, with a fresh, detail-focused engineering perspective.

  • Operations & Support

    The systems, platform, and client relationships that keep QuantiDawn fast and reliable.

Why Philippines-Based

The Philippines has a deep bench of construction and engineering talent - we combine that with Australian-hours availability, so you get expert work without the timezone lag or the premium pricing.
